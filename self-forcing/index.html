self-forcing — a fast video model trained on its own frames

A fast video model is usually taught by watching the slow model, frame after frame. The first frame comes out close. Each later frame is then a little wrong about a picture that was already a little wrong, so the error piles up and the clip drifts. Training it on frames it produced itself is noisier, and it stops the pile-up: every frame keeps just the error of a single step. With no error in that step, both ways stay flat.

Frame n under teacher forcing carries n × step error. Every self-forced frame carries step.

Frame 1 matches. Later frames drift only on the teacher-forced train. A step of 0 leaves both flat.
