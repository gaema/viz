forward-pass -- one prompt → the next token, end to end

This runs a whole small model from end to end, rather than taking one part of it apart on its own. A few words go in, get looked up as rows of numbers, compare themselves against each other, pass one at a time through a small two-layer network, and at the end every word the model knows is given a score and one of them is picked. The strip down the left side is the through-line: each stage reads it, works out a small correction, and adds that back rather than overwriting it. The model here is deliberately tiny and its numbers are random, so the word it predicts is meaningless — what is real is the sequence of steps, and that sequence is what runs every time you press enter.

Interactive page: the capstone. Every other page shows one mechanism in isolation; this one chains them, running a short prompt all the way through a tiny (but real) transformer to predict the next token. Anchor: synthesis of Families A–G; Family H.

What it shows

A toy transformer — d=8, 1 layer, 2 heads, gated MLP, tied lm-head, random weights — runs end to end, and you watch the same residual stream transform at each stage (the strip on the left is the through-line):

  1. tokenize — prompt words → integer IDs (vocab indices).
  2. embed — X₀[i] = E[id[i]] + position[i] (a table lookup + a positional offset) → the residual stream.
  3. attention — softmax(QKᵀ/√dₕ) (causal) · V, projected by Wₒ added back: X₁ = X₀ + attnOut. The [n×n] attention matrix is shown.
  4. gated MLP — down( silu(x·W_g) ⊙ (x·W_u) ), added back: X₂ = X₁ + mlp.
  5. lm-head — the last token's final (normed) hidden is projected onto every vocab embedding → one logit per word.
  6. sample — softmax(logits / T) → the next token.

The recurring idea — every block reads the stream, computes a correction, adds it back (the residual stream) — is made literal: the left strip shows X₀ → X₁ → X₂ as you step.

The weights are random, so the predicted word is arbitrary — this page is about the mechanism, not a trained model's answer.

Step the transport (or play) to walk the six stages; drag a prompt token ↕ to swap its word and watch the whole pipeline recompute; tune temperature and the attention head; hover any tensor.
